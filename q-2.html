Popular Healthy Recipes

Nutritious meal ideas with ingredients and calorie information.

BREAKFAST
⏱ 10 minutes 🔥 ~300 kcal

Fruit & Oatmeal Bowl

A fiber-rich breakfast with oats, milk and fresh fruit.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup milk of choice
  • ½ banana and berries
  • 1 teaspoon chia seeds

Nutrition (approx.)

300Calories
12 gProtein
48 gCarbs
8 gFat
View Preparation

Cook the oats with milk. Add sliced banana, berries and chia seeds. Serve warm.

LUNCH
⏱ 15 minutes 🔥 ~350 kcal

Rainbow Vegetable Salad

A colorful combination of fresh vegetables and chickpeas.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mixed salad vegetables
  • ½ cup cooked chickpeas
  • ½ cucumber and 1 tomato
  • Lemon juice and olive oil

Nutrition (approx.)

350Calories
12 gProtein
40 gCarbs
16 gFat
View Preparation

Chop the vegetables, add cooked chickpeas and dress with lemon juice and olive oil.

DINNER
⏱ 25 minutes 🔥 ~420 kcal

Vegetable Rice Bowl

A balanced bowl of rice, vegetables and protein-rich lentils.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked brown rice
  • ½ cup cooked lentils
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • Spices and 1 teaspoon oil

Nutrition (approx.)

420Calories
17 gProtein
68 gCarbs
9 gFat
View Preparation

Cook the rice and lentils. Sauté vegetables with spices and serve everything together.

SNACKS
⏱ 5 minutes 🔥 ~200 kcal

Fresh Fruit & Nut Mix

A simple snack with fruit, nuts and natural nutrients.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium apple
  • 10 almonds
  • 1 small orange
  • Water as desired

Nutrition (approx.)

200Calories
5 gProtein
30 gCarbs
9 gFat
View Preparation

Wash and slice the fruit. Serve with almonds for a convenient snack.

SMALL CHANGES, BIG BENEFITS

Healthy Eating Tips

Build simple habits that support long-term wellbeing.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day and more when needed.

🥦

Eat More Vegetables

Include a variety of colorful vegetables and fruits in your meals.

🌾

Choose Whole Grains

Try oats, whole-wheat roti, millets and brown rice.

🍳

Include Protein

Choose dal, beans, eggs, dairy, tofu or other suitable protein foods.

🧂

Limit Excess Salt

Reduce heavily salted packaged foods and season meals thoughtfully.

⏰

Eat Mindfully

Enjoy your food slowly and pay attention to hunger and fullness.
